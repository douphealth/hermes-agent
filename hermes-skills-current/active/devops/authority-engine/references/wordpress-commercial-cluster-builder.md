# WordPress Commercial Cluster Builder

## Purpose
Turn scattered posts into a connected commercial SEO cluster with clearer authority flow and revenue logic.

## Cluster model
Each cluster should define:
- hub page
- money pages
- support/how-to pages
- comparison pages
- troubleshooting pages
- trust pages
- FAQ / answer-box opportunities
- next-step CTA paths
- freshness/update ownership
- KPI ownership for rankings, CTR, conversions, and authority growth

## Workflow
1. Identify the main commercial or traffic hub.
2. Map adjacent support intents.
3. Map required entities, modifiers, objections, and subtopics so coverage becomes comprehensive rather than random.
4. Define parent/child links.
5. Upgrade money pages first.
6. Add support posts that answer real adjacent queries.
7. Standardize related-guides blocks.
8. Add next-step CTAs that move readers deeper.
9. Reinforce trust pages from commercial pages.
10. Optimize relevant category/hub pages too.
11. Ensure each serious page has enough internal links and at least one extraction-friendly answer asset if the SERP warrants it.
12. Verify cluster links live.

For hub/taxonomy design, also load `wordpress-category-hub-architecture`.

## Minimum page requirements inside a commercial cluster
- clear H1
- direct intro
- trust note where needed
- related guides block
- next-step CTA
- at least one parent or sibling link
- one logical conversion or continuation action
- when relevant, a snippet / AEO asset such as a definition block, steps, checklist, comparison table, or FAQ
- enough topical specificity that the page strengthens the cluster instead of duplicating it

## Anti-patterns
- isolated posts with no cluster links
- random blog output with no revenue path
- listicles with no next step
- weak trust pages not linked from money pages
- duplicate near-identical pages competing for the same term

## Output contract
Report:
- cluster hub
- member pages
- internal link pattern
- trust pages used
- what authority / AEO / GEO gaps the cluster now covers
- next content gaps to fill
- freshness / KPI review cadence
