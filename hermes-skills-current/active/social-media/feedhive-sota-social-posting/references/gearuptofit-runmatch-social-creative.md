# GearUpToFit RunMatch AI social creative notes

Use when creating IG/FB posts for `https://gearuptofit.com/shoe-finder/`.

## Strategic decision

For GearUpToFit tools, promote **RunMatch AI Shoe Finder first** as a mini-campaign before broader tool-bundle posts. It has the clearest buyer intent and best visual/social hook: runners understand the pain of choosing the wrong shoes immediately.

Recommended sequence:
1. Broad hook / scroll-stop: `STOP BUYING / RUNNING SHOES / BLIND`
2. Tool explanation: phone/AI shoe-match card + shoes
3. Budget angle: `DON'T OVERPAY FOR / THE WRONG SHOES`
4. Terrain angle: `ROAD, TRAIL, GYM? / YOUR SHOES SHOULD MATCH`
5. Support/injury angle: `YOUR FEET NEED / THE RIGHT SUPPORT`

## Visual direction

Best-performing concept from session QA:
- 1080x1080 square for IG/FB feed.
- Dark/orange GearUpToFit palette.
- Generic unbranded running shoes on road/trail/athletic surface.
- Abstract smartphone/AI shoe-match card on the right to clarify this is a tool, not a shoe ad.
- Big left-side headline with `BLIND` in orange.
- CTA button low-left but with safe bottom margin.

## Quality pitfalls

- Avoid Nike swoosh-like marks, Adidas three-stripe marks, or any brand-like shoe design. AI-generated shoe marks can create trademark confusion and weaken independent-review trust.
- Remove tiny fake labels/text from shoe tongues, soles, phone UI, or cards.
- A dramatic shoe-only image can scroll-stop but may look like a shoe ad. For conversion, include a subtle tool cue: phone, match card, shoe profile, terrain/mileage signal.
- Do not use long URLs on the image. Put the link in the caption.
- If the image is good enough, stop regenerating. Ship and test instead of chasing tiny changes.
