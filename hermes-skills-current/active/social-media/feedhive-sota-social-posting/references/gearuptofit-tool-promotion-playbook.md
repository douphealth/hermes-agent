# GearUpToFit tool/app promotion playbook

Use when promoting GearUpToFit's free tools instead of single review posts.

## Priority URLs
- Homepage/tools hub: `https://gearuptofit.com/`
- RunMatch AI Shoe Finder: `https://gearuptofit.com/shoe-finder/`
- WatchMatch AI: `https://gearuptofit.com/watch-match/`
- Body Recomp OS Fitness Planner: `https://gearuptofit.com/fitness-plan/`

## Strategic angle
Promote GearUpToFit as a fitness decision platform: free tools that help users choose shoes, choose watches, and build training plans. This is stronger brand/organic traffic positioning than isolated affiliate review promotion.

## First ecosystem post
Image overlay:
```text
STOP GUESSING
YOUR FITNESS GEAR

Free tools to find:
• Running shoes
• Fitness watches
• Training plan

GEARUPTOFIT
```
CTA: `TRY THE FREE TOOLS →`

GPT Images base prompt:
```text
Create a premium 1080x1080 square social media base image for a modern fitness technology website.

Scene: A clean premium fitness desk/gym setup showing three items: a pair of modern running shoes, a smartwatch/fitness watch, and a simple workout notebook or training plan card. Place them on a dark matte surface with subtle gym texture in the background. The mood should communicate smart fitness decisions, gear comparison, and personalized training.

Composition: Leave a large clean dark negative-space area on the left side for headline text overlay. Place the running shoes, smartwatch, and workout plan elements on the right side and lower-right third. The image should feel balanced, premium, and uncluttered.

Style: Photorealistic commercial fitness product photography, high-end editorial lighting, dark charcoal and graphite tones, warm orange accent highlights, crisp realistic textures, shallow depth of field, modern fitness-tech aesthetic, trustworthy and premium.

Important: No text, no letters, no logos, no brand names, no labels, no watermarks, no app UI, no fake screen text, no icons, no distorted objects, no malformed shoes, no weird watch display.

Output: Square 1:1, ultra high resolution, sharp, professional commercial photography.
```

## 4-post sequence
1. Ecosystem/hub: `STOP GUESSING / YOUR FITNESS GEAR` → homepage + all tools.
2. Shoe Finder: `YOUR RUNNING SHOES / SHOULD MATCH YOUR BODY` → `/shoe-finder/`.
3. Watch Match: `DON'T BUY THE WRONG / FITNESS WATCH` → `/watch-match/`.
4. Fitness Plan: `BUILD YOUR 8-WEEK / FITNESS PLAN` → `/fitness-plan/`.

## Platform notes
- IG feed and FB feed can share the 1080x1080 asset.
- Do not put long URLs in the image; captions carry URLs.
- Facebook caption can include all tool links and fewer hashtags.
- Instagram caption should be tighter and save/share oriented.
