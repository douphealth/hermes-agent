# Cluster Role Matrix

## Define
Purpose: explain the topic cleanly
Typical formats: pillar, glossary, intro hub

## Compare
Purpose: evaluate options and tradeoffs
Typical formats: vs, alternatives, best-for, comparison table pages

## Persuade
Purpose: move users toward a commercial action
Typical formats: money pages, service pages, buying guides

## Troubleshoot
Purpose: resolve blockers, mistakes, objections
Typical formats: how-to, mistakes, why pages, issue pages

## Qualify
Purpose: help users self-segment by fit
Typical formats: use-case pages, audience-specific pages

## Route
Purpose: move authority and users to the right next page
Typical formats: hubs, category pages, roundup bridges

## Convert
Purpose: capture lead/action/revenue step
Typical formats: landing pages, lead-magnet pages, core offer pages
