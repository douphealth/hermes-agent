# Observation Window Guide

## 1-2 weeks
Use for:
- live on-page adjustments
- quick-answer additions
- internal-link reinforcement on active pages

## 2-6 weeks
Use for:
- money-page upgrades
- cluster-support wave effects
- snippet/AIO capture attempts

## 1-3 months
Use for:
- broad cluster authority shifts
- trust-layer effects
- site-section re-prioritization

## Notes
- do not overreact too early on slow-moving pages
- do not wait forever on pages with obvious underperformance
- tie review timing to the scale of the change
