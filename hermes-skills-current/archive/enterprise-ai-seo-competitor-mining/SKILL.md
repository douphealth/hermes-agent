# Enterprise AI SEO Competitor Mining

Use this when the user wants stronger SEO strategy, faster opportunity discovery, or a more enterprise-grade content operating system.

For cross-layer orchestration that combines this with page rewrites, AEO, GEO, and cluster execution, load `devops/authority-engine` first.

## Core insight
Do not stop at obvious competitors.
Use SERP overlap to discover the real competitors already ranking across your revenue and topic queries.
The winning move is to flip the search from keywords -> domains, then from domains -> content gaps.

## Principles
1. Revenue-first beats traffic vanity.
2. Hidden competitors matter more than famous competitors.
3. Do not copy wording; copy demand geometry.
4. Build clusters around intent, entities, and conversion paths.
5. AI is for synthesis, prioritization, and brief generation — not for hallucinated facts.
6. Trust, evidence, internal-link architecture, and answer extraction are part of SEO, not separate work.
7. A winning opportunity model must account for blue-link rankings, snippet capture, AI Overview citation, and brand/entity visibility together.

## Enterprise workflow

### 1. Build the query ladder
Start from one broad term in the niche, then fan it out into:
- broader category terms
- problem-aware terms
- solution-aware terms
- commercial/comparison terms
- modifiers by audience, industry, tool, geography, and urgency
- informational support terms

Minimum useful set:
- 5 head terms
- 10 mid-tail terms
- 10 high-intent modifiers
- 10 support questions

### 2. Run SERP-overlap competitor discovery
For each query bucket, capture the top ranking URLs/domains.
Then normalize to root domains and score domains by:
- total appearances across the query set
- average position
- presence on high-intent queries
- presence on multiple intent classes

A strong hidden competitor is a domain that:
- appears repeatedly across your target SERPs
- ranks on valuable modifiers
- was not in the user's initial competitor list

This is the crucial flip:
- first search keywords
- then search which domains dominate them
- then analyze those domains as the real competitive set

### 3. Build the opportunity matrix
For each target query or topic, capture:
- primary intent: informational / commercial / transactional / navigational
- buyer stage
- likely page type needed: landing page, comparison, template, listicle, tool, category, glossary, tutorial, statistics, FAQ
- monetization path
- trust requirement: citations, author proof, methodology, disclosure, examples
- snippet opportunity: paragraph, list, table, steps, FAQ, video, comparison
- AI Overview likelihood and likely extractable sections
- entity set and subtopics that must be covered for authority
- internal-link parent and child pages
- content asset needed: calculator, downloadable checklist, lead magnet, comparison table, FAQ block
- freshness burden / update triggers

### 4. Extract page-pattern intelligence
From the true competitors, identify recurring winning patterns:
- comparison pages
- alternatives pages
- use-case pages
- template libraries
- statistics pages
- programmatic location or industry pages
- calculators / tools
- review methodology / editorial trust pages

Do not just ask “what keywords do they rank for?”
Also ask:
- what page formats keep recurring?
- what trust elements appear near the top?
- which internal links are repeated sitewide?
- what conversion asset sits beside the ranking page?

### 5. Use AI for synthesis, not blind generation
Generate an AI content spec per page containing:
- dominant search intent
- secondary intents to satisfy without diluting the page
- must-cover entities, comparisons, objections, and subquestions
- missing angles competitors ignore
- proof requirements and citations needed
- snippet patterns to target
- AI Overview / GEO-ready answer blocks to include
- internal links in and out
- recommended schema
- CTA and conversion asset
- update cadence / freshness triggers

Every spec should be strong enough that a writer can produce:
- an answer-first intro
- a section stack aligned to SERP winners
- at least one extractable list / table / definition / FAQ block when appropriate
- information gain rather than paraphrased competitor mush

### 6. Prioritize by business value
Score every opportunity by:
- expected commercial value
- SERP weakness / gap size
- internal-link leverage
- content production cost
- trust burden
- speed to publish

A high-value topic with moderate volume and direct monetization usually beats a flashy high-volume vanity keyword.

### 7. Turn research into a publishable cluster
The minimum enterprise cluster includes:
- 1 money page or commercial hub
- 3 to 5 supporting use-case or comparison pages
- 3 to 5 informational support pages
- 1 trust asset: editorial policy, methodology, disclosure, author page, or evidence page
- 1 conversion asset: lead magnet, tool, checklist, template, calculator, newsletter CTA
- internal-link rules that ensure each serious page has parent, sibling, next-step, and trust routing
- freshness and measurement ownership so top pages get reviewed on a predictable cadence

### 8. Close the loop after publishing
After launch, verify:
- the page is indexable
- the internal links are live
- the schema exists and is accurate
- the CTA and conversion path work
- the page is linked from at least one relevant parent page
- the snippet, title, and on-page heading align with intent
- the page contains at least one clean extraction target if the SERP suggests snippet or AIO potential
- measurement hooks exist for ranking, CTR, conversions, and AI citation tracking

## AI SEO rules that prevent slop
- Never invent stats, case studies, or outcomes.
- Do not publish generic “complete guide” filler unless the SERP truly wants it.
- Match the dominant page type before trying to out-write the market.
- Add information gain: better comparisons, sharper frameworks, stronger examples, clearer trust signals, or better UX.
- If the query is commercial, make the conversion path explicit.
- If the query is YMYL or trust-sensitive, increase evidence and author transparency before scaling output.

## Deliverables
Produce these artifacts whenever possible:
1. Real competitor set by SERP overlap
2. Opportunity matrix
3. Prioritized content cluster
4. AI content brief per target page
5. Internal-link map
6. Trust-asset checklist
7. Conversion-path recommendation
8. snippet / AEO target list
9. GEO / AI citation opportunity notes
10. KPI / refresh cadence recommendation

## Verification checklist
- hidden competitors identified from SERP overlap, not guessed
- opportunities grouped by intent and business value
- each recommended page has a defined page type and CTA
- trust requirements called out explicitly
- internal-link relationships mapped
- proposed topics are differentiated instead of duplicative

## Output contract
Report:
- seed query set used
- hidden competitors discovered
- highest-priority gaps
- recommended page/cluster plan
- trust and conversion assets required
- what should be executed next
